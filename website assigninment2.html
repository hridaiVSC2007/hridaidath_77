Welcome to Computer Science Fundamentals

This course introduces the basic concepts of computer science, including
problem solving, programming, and web technologies. It is designed for
first-year students who are new to the subject.

❔  Frequently Asked Questions

What is this course?
It is an introductory computer science course covering programming, computers, web development and problem solving.

Who can take this course?
It is designed for beginners and first-year students.

Do I need programming experience?
No. The course starts with basic concepts.
